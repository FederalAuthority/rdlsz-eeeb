Tropical Roleplay Staff Application

We appreciate your interest in becoming a part of our Staff Team. Before applying, ensure you meet the requirements and understand the guidelines below.

Requirements

  • You must be 14 or older.
  • Prior staffing experience on servers is a plus.
  • You must be able to uphold a professional and mature attitude.
  • You must not currently hold another position on another server.
  • You must have a clean punishment record (no punishments in the last 2 weeks).
  • You must be active within our server and community.

Guidelines

  • Put as much effort and detail into your answers as possible.
  • Learn and memorize our rules to better answer scenario questions.
  • Be honest; lying on your application will get you nowhere.
  • Do not ask staff about the status of your application while it is under review.

We wish you the best of luck on your application!
