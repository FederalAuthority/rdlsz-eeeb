RULES & STANDARDS

Welcome to Tropical RP. Review these rules carefully; they are designed to maintain a positive and professional environment. Staff maintain final authority.

Operational Support

Use /calladmin in-game for assistance. Reports **must** have a video clip to be actionable.

Discord Community Standards

• Adhere to Discord's TOS.
• English is the only permitted language.
• Prohibited: NSFW, controversial topics, sexist, phobic, or racist remarks.
• Mature behavior is required; malicious behavior results in immediate bans.
• No self-promotion or unwanted DMs. No poaching (advertising other servers).

Zero Tolerance

Discrimination & Slurs Permanent Ban

Racism of any kind is prohibited. Includes N-word (Hard ER). Soft 'a' is allowed in RP only. Excessive use results in permanent removal.

Homophobic Remarks (F-Slur) Permanent Ban

Remarks targeting the LGBTQIA+ community are disrespectful and strictly prohibited.

Out-of-Character Threats Permanent Ban

Includes DDOS, DOX, Death Threats, or telling someone to "KYS". Applies to Game and Discord.

Modding / Exploiting Permanent Ban

Attempting to bypass anticheat or spawning explosive weapons without a proper role.

In-Game Violations

RDM / VDM Warning → Kick → Ban

Shooting or running over players without a valid RP reason. **Mass RDM/VDM (3+ people) is an immediate ban.**

Fail RP / Value of Life Warning → Kick → Ban

Failing to fear for your life. Examples: ignoring guns pointed at you, self-reviving during active scenes, or walking away from major accidents unharmed.

Staff Evasion (LTAP) Ban

Leaving the server during an active scene or staff situation to avoid punishment.

Driving Standards

Unrealistic / GTA Driving Warning → Temp Ban

Driving 300MPH down Joshua, off-roading lowriders, or driving without care for life.

10-80 Speed Limit (250 MPH) Scene 22'ed + Warning

Driving over 250 MPH in a pursuit ruins balance for LEO. Disciplinary action will follow.

Player Interactions

NLR (New Life Rule): If you die, you lose all memory of previous events. You cannot return to the scene for 15 minutes.

Meta-Gaming: Using OOC info for an in-game advantage is strictly prohibited.

Cop Baiting: Purposely committing crimes to initiate chases without RP reason is prohibited.

Scene Interference: You cannot interfere with ongoing LEO arrests or traffic stops.

Powergaming: Using game mechanics to gain an unrealistic advantage or glitching out of scenarios.

Policies & Support

Second Chance Policy: Appeals are eligible for two unbans. **Bans for Hard-R slurs or KYS threats are never eligible for appeal.**

Peacetime: While active, all RP must be passive. No shooting allowed.

Donation Support: Enhancements are available via Tebex. DM Marley or Lamb with proof of purchase.
