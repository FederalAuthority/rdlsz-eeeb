Code of Conduct

RULES & STANDARDS

Welcome to Tropical RP. Our community is built on mutual respect and high-standard roleplay. Staff maintain final authority on all rulings.

Operational Support

Use /calladmin in-game for immediate assistance. Reports require valid video evidence to be actionable by Internal Affairs.

Zero Tolerance

Discrimination & Slurs Permanent Ban

Racism, homophobia, or hate speech of any kind is strictly prohibited. This includes slurs (Hard ER). Soft 'a' is permitted only within organic RP; excessive use is punishable.

Out-of-Character Threats Permanent Ban

Includes DOX, DDOS, death threats, or encouraging self-harm. This policy extends to all server-affiliated platforms.

General Violations

RDM & VDM Warn → Kick → Ban

Executing players or using vehicles as weapons without valid RP escalation. Mass RDM (3+) results in an immediate permanent ban.

Fail RP / Value of Life Warning → Ban

Failure to fear for your life or ignoring realistic consequences (e.g., self-reviving during active scenes or ignoring major collisions).

Driving & Realism

Non-Realistic Operation

Off-roading in supercars or driving 200MPH through heavy pedestrian areas without reason is prohibited.

Pursuit Limit: Driving over 250 MPH during 10-80 scenarios is prohibited to maintain LEO engagement balance.

Appeals

Second Chance Policy

Appeals are handled via Discord tickets. Most players are eligible for two "free" unbans. However, bans involving slurs or OOC threats are non-appealable.
